Interpretation

What the data says about Tonies & games per device

📈
At the same box age, TB2 G1 boxes have activated more than TB1 boxes: after 6 months 7.8 vs 6.5 Tonies & games per box (+21%), after 10 months 9.6 vs 7.7 (+25%). Lifetime values (15.5 per TB1 box vs 8.1 per TB2 G1 box) mainly reflect the age of the fleets: most G1 boxes were activated only since Dec 2025. Older ages only contain older cohorts, so the curves also reflect the cohort mix.
🧸
Oct 2026: per active box TB1 activates 0.29 Tonies & games a month (mature fleet), TB2 G1 0.85. TB2 G2 (3.46) and TBLite (2.49) are much higher, most likely because their fleets consist mostly of brand-new boxes that are still filling up with their first Tonies. My First Tonies are 11.9% of TB2 G1 activations vs 2.6% on TB1.
🎮
Games: Tonieplay games are only activated on TB2 boxes (TB2 G1: 36,203 in Oct 2026 = 2.8% of its activations; TB2 G2: 2,305 = 1.7%). Households that started on a TB1 have activated 319,988 games so far, an upsale that happens on a later-added TB2 box (about 0.04 games per TB1-first household vs 0.17 per TB2 G1-first household).
⚠️
Reading notes: Content Tonies are the bulk of all activations (87% on TB1 boxes over the lifetime). Age curves use complete months of life only; TB2 G1 is shown for months 1–10 and TB2 G2 / TBLite for months 1–2, because later ages contain too few boxes (mostly early-launch boxes). Types come from the product hierarchy: Content, My First, Clever, Creative, Book, Games (Tonieplay games and enabler sets) and Other (plush, nightlights, sets, other).

Interpretation

What the data says about the daily rhythm

🌅
Morning peak (7–9h): Wake-up routine. For TB1 and TB2 G1, 7h and 8h are nearly level (TB1 15.6M / 15.5M sessions, G1 peaks at 8h).
🌙
Evening peak (18–20h) is the strongest block: 29% of all TB1 sessions fall within 18–21h. Evening sessions are long (bedtime stories): 19–21h deliver 35% of TB1 minutes and 25% of G1 minutes.
🔄
Hardware type slightly influences timing: TB1 is more evening-heavy (29% vs 25% for TB2 G1), TB2 G1 uses late morning and afternoon more strongly (17% and 20% vs 14% and 16%). TB2 G2 and TBLite follow the G1 pattern with the evening peak already at 18h. On weekends (Sat/Sun) the late-morning hours are clearly stronger, and on Sunday the most boxes are active.
